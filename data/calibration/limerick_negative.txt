# Negative Control Limericks for Calibration
# 5 deliberately weak limericks: correct 5-line AABBA structure
# and roughly correct syllable counts, but bland, cliched, or
# incoherent content.
# Used to verify the fitness pipeline separates quality tiers.

# Bland report — no wit
There once was a man with a hat
Who sat in a chair and just sat
He looked at the wall
He did nothing at all
And that is the end of all that

# Generic and flat
There once was a girl in a town
Who walked up the street and walked down
She went to the store
And came back for more
And that was her life in that town

# Incoherent (correct form)
There once was a lamp on a cheese
Who barked at the wind in the trees
The fork ate the door
The ceiling said four
And tables were friends with the breeze

# Maximum cliche
There once was a cat on a mat
Who chased after mouse and a rat
It ran to the right
And then out of sight
And that was the end of the cat

# Emotionally flat
There once was a person named Jane
Who walked in the sun and the rain
She went to her car
She did not drive far
And then she went walking again
