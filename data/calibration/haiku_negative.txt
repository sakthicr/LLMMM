# Negative Control Haikus for Calibration
# 5 deliberately weak haikus: correct 5-7-5 syllable counts
# but bland, cliched, or incoherent content.
# Used to verify the fitness pipeline separates quality tiers.

# Bland weather report
The sun is shining
The sky is very blue now
It is a nice day

# Generic statement
I like to eat food
Food is something that I eat
Eating food is good

# Incoherent nonsense (valid 5-7-5)
Purple dogs can fly
My refrigerator sings
Tables eat the moon

# Maximum cliche
Roses are so red
Violets are very blue
Sugar is so sweet

# Emotionally flat
I woke up today
I went outside to the store
Then I came back home
