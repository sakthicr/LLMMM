# Reference Haikus for Calibration (Section 10)
# ~15 real human haikus from established poets.
# Format: lines starting with # are metadata, poem lines follow.
# Poems separated by blank lines.

# An old silent pond — Matsuo Bashō
An old silent pond
A frog jumps into the pond
Splash! Silence again

# The light of a candle — Yosa Buson
The light of a candle
Is transferred to another candle
Spring twilight

# Over the wintry — Natsume Sōseki
Over the wintry
Forest winds howl in rage
With no leaves to blow

# A world of dew — Kobayashi Issa
A world of dew
And within every dewdrop
A world of struggle

# In the twilight rain — Matsuo Bashō
In the twilight rain
These brilliant hued hibiscus
A lovely sunset

# The old pond — Matsuo Bashō (variant)
The old pond
A frog leaps in
Sound of the water

# First autumn morning — Matsuo Bashō
First autumn morning
The mirror I stare into
Shows my father's face

# A caterpillar — Matsuo Bashō
A caterpillar
This deep in fall
Still not a butterfly

# On a withered branch — Matsuo Bashō
On a withered branch
A crow has alighted
Nightfall in autumn

# The temple bell stops — Matsuo Bashō
The temple bell stops
But I still hear the sound
Coming from the flowers

# Don't weep insects — Kobayashi Issa
Don't weep insects
Lovers stars themselves
Must part

# A summer river — Masaoka Shiki
A summer river being crossed
How pleasing
With sandals in my hands

# Spring rain — Masaoka Shiki
Spring rain
Conveyed under the trees
In drops

# After killing a spider — Masaoka Shiki
After killing a spider
How lonely I feel
In the cold of night

# I write and erase — Kaga no Chiyo
I write and erase
I write and erase and then
A poppy blooms
